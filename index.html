What is Mines?

Mines is an online casino game where Minesweeper-style gameplay reveals mines or bombs with each wager placed on the 5×5 grid.

How to play Mines

Before each round, determine your bet size and decide how many mines on the board you want to set. You can choose to have between 1 and 24 mines hidden on the 25 tiles.

Every safe tile reveals a gem and increases your multiplier. After each successful pick, you can reveal another tile or use Cash Out to lock in your winnings. Selecting a mine ends the round immediately, and the wager is lost.
